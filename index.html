About Me关于我

I am an M.S. student in Data Science at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor. I received my B.Eng. in Data Science and Big Data Technology from Tongji University in July 2025.

In spring 2023, I was an exchange student in the Department of Electrical Engineering at National Taiwan University.

In 2026, I was a Research Intern at Touchstone AI in the Benchmark Research Group, supervised by Hui Shen.

My research interests span efficient multimodal models and visual generation, including speculative decoding, streaming video agents, remote sensing image restoration, and Chinese character modeling.
